The Beginning

A single hearth,
an unchanging vow

It began in 1965 in the historic city of Kuchaman, Rajasthan — a single shop, a small family of karigars, and one absolute conviction: that Indian mithai made with pure desi ghee and unhurried care would speak for itself.

Decades passed. Celebrations changed. But Keshuji Sweets stayed true — slow-cooking mawa in brass kadhais, roasting lentils to golden aroma, and hand-crafting the same heirloom recipes that first delighted Rajasthan's families over half a century ago.

01
Pure Desi Ghee

No hydrogenated oils, no shortcuts. Every sweet begins with pure clarified butter from trusted dairies — the authentic way.

02
Handcrafted Daily

Our mithai is made fresh every morning. Small batches, hands-on preparation. Nothing sits on a shelf overnight.

03
Heritage Recipes

Our recipes were perfected before most of us were born. Passed down through hands, refined through decades of mastery.

04
Seasonal & Honest

We honor each season: Ghevar in Monsoon, Gajar Halwa in Winter, Shahi Gujiya for Holi. Tradition, beautifully respected.
